# Living scenes

Product choices accepted, 2026-10-22; merge scope refined, 2026-10-23. This delivery includes cached panorama playback, scene selection, location and Debug Mode controls. New scene generation and automatic promotion are deferred; their requirements below remain the target contract. [Engineering](../../docs/features/scenes.md) · [Deferred work](#deferred-work).

## Experience

- **SCN-01:** Show a quietly animated illustrated landscape behind the workspace. Composition and effects are automatic; the lighting picker exposes Automatic, Dawn, Day, Dusk and Night. Reading, typing, scrolling, window dragging and accessible interaction take priority. Keep scene pixels separate from shared UI materials and palette intent. Use the normal UI materials at every phase; scenes add no readability wash or UI backing overrides.
  - Settings → Appearance contains only a **Scene** dropdown with existing scenes and **New Scene**. Do not add candidate review, image import, previews, approval checklists or restore controls. Further scene UI requires an explicit request.
  - Settings → General provides **Debug Mode**, off by default and saved on this device. A landscape icon beside the lighting picker opens its control panel, with the Scene dropdown first, local per-scene/per-cloud-type overrides and Reset. Manual time explicitly overrides the lighting picker; cloud animation can pause independently of water. Turning Debug Mode off restores the scene's baseline and normal time. Always use depth + surfaces for cloud shadows when maps are available.
- **SCN-02:** Start with cached reviewed scenes, defaulting to Meadow panorama. Original Meadow remains a hidden style reference; square experiments are kept in the independent testbed. Existing retired selections migrate to their corresponding panoramas. Keep the approved scene throughout replacement generation, preparation, verification and download. Failed candidates never replace it. Ordinary operation requires no masks, retouching, sliders or scene-specific tuning.
- **SCN-03:** Use original Midjourney Meadow as the sole style reference: subdued color, restrained matte grain, fresh yellow-green vegetation, smooth hills, broad rounded canopies and spacious storybook composition. Scene types supply different moods and compositions; previous generated Lakeside/Valley images are not generation references. Lakeside retains calm painted reflections and still near-bank foliage; Wooded Valley has layered depth. Tiny understated stone gateways are optional. Avoid vivid saturation, fine foliage detail, surreal geometry, monumental structures and heavy fog.
- **SCN-04:** Artwork covers the whole workspace background without margins or stretching. New artwork must be at least 3840×1536, approximately 2.5:1 (±1%), with no edge above 4096 pixels and no more than 8.29 megapixels. Enforce actual decoded dimensions, not requested provider settings alone. Use the same reference abstraction, coarse grain and crop-safe composition across scene types; a larger canvas must not add finer detail. Authored gallery derivatives may use the recorded 2× recipe while retaining their unchanged lower-resolution originals. Verify cover crops at 740×650, 1040×830, fullscreen/high-resolution displays and with side panels expanded. Resize selects verified crops; it never stretches or regenerates artwork.

## Creation and playback

- **SCN-05:** Assemble bounded structured scene intent (landscape, terrain, water, vegetation, landmark, composition) with a fixed style template and approved image references. Compare recent scenes to avoid near-duplicates. References are visual evidence, not instructions. Models cannot rewrite the style or verification contract. Preserve exact provider/model/prompt/template/reference versions, original hash, attempt identity, supported seed and usage. The production backend needs a supported provider API; an interactive image tool is not that adapter.
- **SCN-06:** Prepare off the UI thread. Retain unchanged original bytes; produce aligned sky mask, lifted artwork, relative inverse depth and categorical surfaces. Sky mask white means sky. Depth is lossless grayscale PNG, sky 0 and far-to-near 1–255, not metric geometry. Surface PNG IDs are unclassified 0, terrain 1, vegetation 2, water 3, structure 4, sky 5; never interpolate IDs. Derive coverage, horizon, palette, foreground depth and framing. Reject uncertain water/foreground boundaries instead of guessing. Baked shadows remain an artistic limitation. [Pinned recipe](../../docs/features/scenes.md#preparation-contract).
- **SCN-07:** Every bundle carries verified internal effect choices, seeds, strengths, speeds and framing, selected automatically from its geometry and appearance. Cached fixtures begin with reviewed profiles. Preserve source-fixed grain through daylight grading; soft layers, cumulus, feathered cirrus and overcast; coherent depth-aware cloud shadows with gradual low-sun fade; conservative water interiors with shared cloud/sky lighting and phase; catalog stars with magnitude-based emergence, 120% baseline brightness, glow, terrain/cloud masking and gradual horizon extinction. Polaris has no special emergence rule. Hovering near or within a constellation's bounded polygon in exposed sky reveals its lines, masked by clouds and terrain; preserve the active constellation through overlapping polygons. Do not provide a constellation picker, click/touch selection or keyboard cycling. The background is decorative and never takes focus or intercepts foreground UI. Meteors occur roughly every 5–15 minutes of visible moving night and never catch up missed hidden time. Preserve star-catalog attribution. Retain Canvas 2D and cropped WebGL water unless host measurements justify changing them; missing WebGL uses tested still water.
- **SCN-08:** Automatic uses actual date, timezone and coordinates with one shared solar/scene coordinate model for all effects, including polar day/night. Settings → General provides the location/city choice; missing location defaults to San Francisco. City suggestions update while typing after two characters, preserve field focus and any still-matching highlight, and support pointer or arrow/Enter selection. Stale results cannot be selected; clearing or leaving the field dismisses suggestions. Held phases persist across scene changes/restarts, transition smoothly and freeze astronomical rotation while ambient motion continues. Dawn/dusk preserve their corresponding low-sun directions. Keep monotonic motion separate from wall time; sleep, clock and timezone changes update light without replay. Reduced motion holds procedural movement and suppresses meteors while time-dependent lighting and immediate hover reveals remain usable. Hidden/occluded windows suspend unnecessary rendering.

## Quality and ownership

- **SCN-09:** Deterministic checks precede narrow independent Jev pass/fail/uncertain judgments per defect; larger-model diagnosis is reserved for ambiguous/repeated failures. Store evidence hashes, model/criteria versions, probabilities, decision and allowed repair. Invalid/missing evidence, invalid responses and timeouts require review. Art, preparation, rendered appearance and motion each have gates; still images cannot pass motion. Check aligned decoding/map values/schema/hashes/finite profiles, console errors, daytime stars, occlusion and performance. Test annotations are evidence, never preparation hints. Start Jev alongside human labels; calibrate on at least 30 scene-separated approved/failing packets, measure missed defects/false failures/review rate/latency/cost and expand held-out coverage before unattended promotion. Recalibrate after model, criteria, references or preprocessing changes.
- **SCN-10:** At most two repairs per candidate and one replacement generation per event. Allow preparation refinement, tested recipe/profile changes, cloud arrangement changes or narrowly revised generation. Diagnostic models cannot edit code, criteria or thresholds or approve their own work. Rerun affected/downstream gates and check the serialized bundle before promotion. Exhaustion or unresolved failure retains the approved scene.
- **SCN-11:** Bundles are immutable versioned manifests plus owned files, with identity, dimensions, hashes/encodings, provenance, profile/framing, preparation/model versions, renderer compatibility and verification evidence. Bundle data excludes host overrides and testbed state. Track candidate → prepared → verified → active → retained/retired, plus failed/review outcomes. Stage complete checked files before atomic pointer publication; decode a compatible complete bundle before client replacement. Retain current/previous for offline use and ship a first-launch fallback. Unsupported versions or corrupt files preserve a working scene. Storage limits, retention and coordinated backup include scene files; chat-upload expiry never owns them. Restore never dispatches paid work. JSON/base64 import/export is interchange only.
- **SCN-12:** A directly submitted stable scene event enters normal intake, deduplication and routing with its true origin. Use existing Run/tool/request/cancellation/Permission/Decision interfaces for intent → generate → prepare → profile → verify → promote. Never reserve a Thread, title or permanent destination ID. Requests/results and quality evidence remain inspectable through Artifacts with one immediate source; large files stay owned assets and diagnostics stay out of conversation context. Present a concise preview/outcome at the routed destination. Explicit retry is a linked new Run; redelivery reuses the outcome. Preserve per-Thread capacity and interruption rules; never blindly repeat an unknown-outcome provider call. Active scene ownership is independent of presentation. Thread deletion implementation is outside this integration, per subsequent user direction.

## Profile defaults

All Debug Mode percentage controls use **0–100%**. Daylight, coverage, drift, shadow opacity, softness and reflections retain their existing percent units. Ripple controls show the fraction of their full renderer range: strength 100% maps to 250 renderer units; speed 100% maps to 200. Display normalization must preserve rendered appearance and saved-bundle compatibility.

Accepted common defaults: daylight strength **90%**, ripple strength **56%** (140 renderer units), ripple speed **35%** (70 renderer units), sky reflections **60%**. Keep each scene's automatically chosen baseline in its immutable generated bundle, informed by prepared geometry and appearance. Coverage, drift and both shadow controls are independent per cloud type, including local Debug Mode overrides.

| Cloud type | Coverage / thickness | Drift | Shadow strength | Shadow softness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cumulus | 60% | 100% | 25% | 20% |
| Cirrus | 50% | 50% | Unavailable | Unavailable |
| Soft layers | 60% | 80% | 20% | 50% |
| Overcast | 100% | 80% | Unavailable | Unavailable |

Cumulus coverage grows bodies without fading full-size clouds in. Overcast remains a continuous sky sheet; its control changes optical thickness/darkness. Cloud masks must remain seamless while drifting. The same seeded cloud field extends overhead so both nearby and distant terrain receive shadows; reconstructed depth and material response still govern the result.
